# Design updates

## Website design

- The site now uses a Yale blue and white striped background, Yale-blue footer, a Gothic display typeface for headings and product descriptions, and a Yale "Y" mark.
- The footer uses bulldog photographs selected for the Home, About us, and remaining site pages, replacing the original CSS bulldog icon.
- Together, these changes make the website more engaging by tying it to the Yale theme, making customers more fully immersed in the website as part of their Yale experience and more likely to buy.

## Chatbot voice

- Each chat opening and new customer request selects a different Yale alumnus from a reviewable list of fifteen writers, artists, journalists, designers, economists, and business leaders. The assistant uses a warm voice inspired by the selected alum while remaining limited to catalogue facts.
- This voice makes the site more engaging by tying the experience to Yale. It should be especially fun for people who might not have known that the person in question was a Yale alumnus, while making the store feel like a more immersive part of their Yale experience and more likely to inspire a purchase.
